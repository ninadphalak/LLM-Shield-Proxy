# Research and publication program

LLM-Shield-Proxy is developing an open evidence base for streaming privacy gateways. The public research program separates practical engineering reports from original security research.

## Current public artifacts

- [Streaming Privacy Gateway Conformance Specification v1.0.0](/docs/conformance/specification-v1)
- [Reproduction guide](/docs/conformance/reproducing)
- [Published and pending results](/docs/conformance/results)
- [Audit evidence-plane status](/docs/evidence-plane-status)
- Apache-2.0 LLM-Shield-Proxy source and a machine-readable report schema

## Technical series

1. **When a token boundary becomes a privacy boundary** - fragmentation threat model, bounded suffix retention, exhaustive partitions, and SSE fidelity.
2. **Signed does not mean immutable** - Ed25519 chain verification, failure semantics, key identity, anchoring, and the WORM boundary.
3. **From vendor benchmark to open conformance lab** - seven-domain specification, reproducible reports, and independent replication.

## Reproduction and citation

Independent results, counterexamples, alternative implementations, and spec issues are welcome. A tagged, DOI-backed release and citation metadata will be published when the first stable multi-host report is complete. Until then, cite the exact repository release or commit used and attach the report checksum.

Self-published explainers will use canonical URLs and will remain distinct from manuscripts under peer or editorial review.
